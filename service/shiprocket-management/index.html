COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our end-to-end shiprocket management services in india workflows, deliverables, and timelines.

How does proactive NDR management reduce RTO rates?

When a delivery attempt fails (customer unavailable, incorrect address, or fake attempt by rider), our team immediately flags the NDR, communicates with the customer via phone/WhatsApp to confirm availability, and schedules an official re-attempt with the courier within 24 hours.

Can Shiprocket be integrated with my Shopify or custom website?

Yes. Shiprocket integrates directly with Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, and custom websites via REST API for seamless real-time order and inventory sync.
