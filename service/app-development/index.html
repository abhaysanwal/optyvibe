COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our custom mobile app development services in india workflows, deliverables, and timelines.

Should I build a native app or a cross-platform app?

For most consumer and business apps, cross-platform frameworks like Flutter or React Native provide 95% single-codebase efficiency, significantly reduced development timelines, and near-native performance. Native development (Swift/Kotlin) is recommended for apps requiring heavy hardware utilization, complex AR features, or deep system-level APIs.

Do you assist with publishing to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store?

Yes. We handle the entire release lifecycle including developer account configuration, certificates, provisioning profiles, asset preparation, and store compliance review.
